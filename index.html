HOW TO USE

0x01

Study Tracker

  1. Open your cert's tracker.
  2. Set a start date and a timeline.
  3. Tick tasks off. Export a backup now and then.

Use it here, or download the .html to run offline. The two keep separate progress.

0x02

Prep Vault

  1. Install Obsidian. It's free.
  2. Download your cert's vault and unzip it.
  3. Open folder as vault, then start on Home.

Download only. Once it's on your machine, it's yours to edit.

0x03

Practice Boxes

  1. Open your cert's practice boxes.
  2. Work from the easiest group down.
  3. Tick each box off and write a mock report.

Also a tab in the tracker. On the site, both share your progress.
