Study Trackers

Interactive, single-file day-by-day study trackers for Hack The Box certifications. Each one turns a certification path into a plan you can actually follow: every module in study order, split into day-sized chunks that match HTB's real section headings, with your progress tracked as you go. Click a cert below to open its tracker, or use the download arrow to save the single HTML file for offline use.

Features

  • Adjustable timeline. Five options set by how many hours a week you can study (about 50, 40, 30, 20, and a relaxed pace). The same curriculum repacks into more or fewer days, and your checked-off progress carries over when you switch.
  • Day-by-day plan. Every module in study order, broken into day-sized chunks that follow HTB's actual section headings, each day with its own tasks and a completion milestone.
  • Progress dashboard. An overall completion ring, days done, best streak, current phase, a weekly progress chart, and an ahead or behind pace indicator based on your start date.
  • Realistic hours. Time estimates come from HTB's own per-module hours, so the day sizes and the weekly-hours math reflect the real workload.
  • Resource library. The official HTB cert and path pages, every module in order, video reviews, first-hand exam write-ups, reporting templates, and the core tooling for each cert.
  • Notes and journal. A collapsible notes field on every day for commands that worked, credentials you found, and things to revisit.
  • Local and offline. One self-contained HTML file with no install, account, or server. Progress saves in your browser, separately per tracker, and Export/Import moves it between machines.

How to use one

  1. Click a cert to open its tracker, or use the download arrow to save the single HTML file for offline use.
  2. Set your start date at the top and pick the timeline that fits your week.
  3. Work through each day and check off tasks. A day is marked complete when all its tasks are done.
  4. Use Export backup now and then to save a copy of your progress.
Hosted vs. downloaded. Using a tracker here in the browser is the easy way to start, and most updates are safe: fixing wording, resources, hours, or timelines keeps your checkmarks in place. But this hosted version can change whenever the site is updated, and a structural change (reordering or adding days or tasks) can shift your saved progress onto the wrong items. If you want a copy that never changes under you, download the HTML file and run it locally, and export a backup regularly either way.
